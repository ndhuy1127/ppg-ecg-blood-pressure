# RQ1 sensitivity simulation (assumptions only, no human data)

CI method of the tables below: `t_envelope` (subject level; the other methods are compared in the sections at the end). Subject draws: 10 separate set(s) per design.

Each row: 2000 simulated studies. `CI width`: mean width of the 95 % subject-level CI of mean d_s (PPG+PAT - PPG-only). `P(U<0)`: share of studies whose CI lies below 0; `P(main)`: share where the CIs of d_s and d0_s (vs zero-change) both lie below 0 (SAP section 6), one outcome alone, at 95 % (no Holm) and at 97.5 % (Holm's first step); SBP and DBP are simulated apart, so the Holm procedure itself is not applied. `inconclusive@δ`: share read as inconclusive at the middle δ. `R3`: median over studies of the R3 statistic (median of per-subject median |ΔBP|); `P2`: median over studies of the P2 statistic (median over subjects of the signed M2 change against same-session M0). All numbers hold only under the assumptions of the config.
